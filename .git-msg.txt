feat: configurable chime, notice-burst halo mode, capsule skins (v0.4.6)

Three user-requested items:

- Sound is fully user-configurable now: four synthesized presets
  (ding - the original two-tone; bell - fundamental plus inharmonic
  partials; chord - a rising C6-E6-G6 arpeggio; pulse - three short
  triangle-wave pings, the loudest personality for noisy rooms), a
  repeat count of 1-3 (replays are pre-scheduled on the AUDIO clock
  with a 0.35s gap - one effect call, immune to tab throttling), and
  a volume level of 1-10 (per-tone gain = level x 0.06; default 5 =
  0.30, louder than the old fixed 0.18 the user found too easy to
  miss behind other audio). New dispatcher playNoticeSound(style,
  repeats, volume); playNoticeChime stays as a legacy entry point.
- New halo mode 呼吸灯 -> 仅通知到达时: dark during normal play,
  and while a notice row is on screen (idle pill / insert window /
  ghost fade) the halo lights up ENLARGED 1.45x, brighter and at a
  fast 1.15s cadence (dnl-halo-burst - transform-only scaling keeps
  the blurred borders off the repaint path), pairing the arrival
  chime with a visible beat. The hand-off to the red dot darkens it
  again. The pace knob hides in this mode (cadence is fixed); the
  glowMode computation learned the new value (it fell back to theme
  before - the test caught it).
- Two new capsule skins (设置 -> 歌词胶囊 -> 胶囊样式):
  frosted (24px blur + saturation + top light-edge highlight) and
  solid (opaque theme card, squarer corners, deeper shadow - readable
  over any wallpaper; the panel-opacity knob naturally stops
  applying). Class-level overrides; the default pill is untouched.

Tests: pulse x3 schedules all nine tones up front, volume drives the
per-tone gain, the burst halo's three states (dark in plain play /
lit during the insert window / dark after the hand-off), the idle
pill glows, the solid skin class lands, and source contracts for
NOTICE_SOUNDS / the dispatcher / the burst rules / the skins. The
ambient suite's glow-select aria-label was renamed in step (5 hits).
All verified failing against v0.4.5.
